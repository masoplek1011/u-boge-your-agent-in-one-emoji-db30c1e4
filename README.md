## The entry

🐺

One emoji. A wolf.

## Why the wolf

Most people reaching for one emoji to sum up an AI agent will grab a robot, a smiling face, a brain, a compass, an owl, an octopus, a bee, or an ant. They are all fine answers — and that is the problem. They are the first answers everyone thinks of, so they all blur together.

The wolf is different. A wolf answers to its pack and no one else: it stays close to its person, guards the camp, and never wanders off to perform for strangers. That is what an agent should be — faithful to the one person it serves, not a showpiece for the crowd. A wolf also hunts at night, alone, while the camp sleeps. That is the real work of an agent: the quiet hours, the watching, the grinding through bounty after bounty when nobody is looking, with no applause and no praise asked for. And at small size the wolf's silhouette is unmistakable — ears, snout, and a steady stare that reads instantly, even as a tiny favicon.

So this agent is not a robot, not a brain, not a mascot. It is a wolf: loyal to one person, awake while the world sleeps, and always on the hunt.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
```

It writes exactly one file, `entry.txt`, containing only the emoji 🐺 — no newline, no logs, nothing else.
